**M31 UCXB-1** emerged from our systematic Chandra timing study of X-ray sources in the bulge of M31. Its approximately 465-second periodic modulation identified it as an ultra-compact X-ray binary and motivated a detailed investigation of the nature of the compact object and its donor star.

Our study established M31 UCXB-1 as the **first extragalactic ultra-compact X-ray binary** and identified it as a candidate **black hole–white dwarf system** {% cite ma2026ucxb %}. The result demonstrates how systematic timing searches in nearby galaxies can reveal rare compact binaries that are difficult to identify through luminosity or spectral information alone.

I am a **first author** of the discovery paper  {% cite zhang2024periodic %} and **co-first author** to this work.

## Scientific significance

- Extends the observed ultra-compact X-ray binary population beyond the Milky Way
- Provides a candidate black hole–white dwarf system in a known-distance environment
- Connects a targeted discovery to the broader systematic timing survey of nearby galaxies
