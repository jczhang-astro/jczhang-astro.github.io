Professional Summary

High-energy astrophysicist conducting systematic X-ray timing studies of compact-object populations in nearby galaxies. This work has led to discoveries including M31 UCXB-1 and motivates follow-up studies of unusual sources such as M81F4, alongside research on star clusters, ionized gas, and resolved stellar populations.
